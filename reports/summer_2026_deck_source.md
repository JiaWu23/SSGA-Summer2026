| M1+M2+M3 ECDF | 4.58% | 0.914 | -7.51% |

These are the same long-only metrics reported in [final_report.md](final_report.md). The key message for the deck is that M1 is the primary trade-selection model, M2 estimates P(success) for candidate trades generated by M1, and M3 sizes those candidates rather than creating new positions. Walk-forward ECDF results are mixed, so the deck should frame ECDF as a risk-control layer rather than a universally superior OOS engine.
